sample-target-app — live metrics

Polling /metrics every 500ms. Point your load generator at any /api/* endpoint and watch these move.

Endpoints:

POST /api/baseline/quick — calibration control, no bottleneck

POST /api/cpu/sync / POST /api/cpu/async — CPU-bound

POST /api/db/query — DB-pool-bound

POST /api/memory/allocate — memory-bound

POST /api/external/call — downstream rate-limit-bound

POST /api/flaky/call — random slowness/failures, load-independent

POST /api/mixed/process — all constraints combined (realistic)
